Clock implementation disclosure
- Enabling PS GEM0 fixes the shared IO PLL at 1000 MHz; that PLL cannot divide exactly to the original 150 MHz FCLK1 request.
- The implemented design therefore uses the design-approved explicit fallback of 125 MHz for the HP0/DMA/AXIS domain.
- The formal gate requires the exact 8.000 ns clock and positive setup/hold slack; it does not accept an implicit 142.857 MHz clock.
- The 100 MHz accelerator control/compute domain is unchanged.
